MindFüll — Support

We usually reply within two working days.

Questions, problems or ideas? Email info@mindfullcounseling.org and tell us what happened and which iPhone and iOS version you're using.

If you're struggling right now

MindFüll is not a crisis service. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). Elsewhere, contact your local emergency number or a helpline near you.

Subscription

  • Cancel or change: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › MindFüll Pro. Or in the app: More › MindFüll Pro.
  • Restore on a new iPhone: open MindFüll and tap Restore on the subscription screen, signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Refunds: handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

  • Moving to a new iPhone: stay signed in to the same iCloud account; your check-ins, journal and goals sync back automatically.
  • Not syncing? Check Settings › your name › iCloud is on, and that iCloud has free space.

Sounds and reminders

  • No sound? Check the ringer switch and volume; sounds keep playing with the screen locked.
  • No reminders? Settings › Notifications › MindFüll › Allow Notifications.
