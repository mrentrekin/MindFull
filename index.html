MindFüll — Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

What you write in MindFüll is yours. There are no MindFüll accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your check-ins, journal and goals stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage — which the developer cannot see.

What MindFüll stores

  • Your entries: mood check-ins (including the tags and notes you add), journal entries, habits, goals and steps, to-dos and lists, which skills you've saved or learned, and a log of the practices you've completed (name, date and length).
  • Your settings: the first name you give it, your chosen background and look, saved sound mixes, and reminder times.
  • A photo, only if you choose one as your background. It is copied into the app's own storage on your device. MindFüll only sees the single photo you pick; it cannot browse your library.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, MindFüll keeps your entries and settings in sync using Apple's CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. This is how your data follows you to a new iPhone. It is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, tied to your Apple ID, and is not accessible to the developer. If you're not signed in to iCloud, everything stays only on your device. Deleting the app removes its data from the device; you can remove the iCloud copy in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Subscriptions

MindFüll Pro is sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details; the developer never receives them. The app only checks with the App Store whether your subscription is active.

Notifications

Reminders you turn on — a daily check-in, area reminders, and a note before a free trial ends — are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent from a server.

Health and wellbeing

MindFüll is a wellbeing tool, not a medical service, and is not a substitute for professional care. It does not read from or write to Apple Health. Your mood and journal entries are never shared with anyone.

Third parties

MindFüll contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no advertising. No data is shared or sold. Links you choose to open (such as a crisis line) leave the app, and that service's own policy applies.

Children

MindFüll is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a future version ever collects anything, this policy will say so plainly before that version ships.
